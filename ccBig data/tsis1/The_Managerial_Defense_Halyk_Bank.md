Go/No-Go Executive Summary
TO: Board of Directors, Halyk Bank
FROM: Head of Cloud Transformation
DATE: 28.01.2026
SUBJECT: Go/No-Go Decision: AI Legal Compliance Assistant for Risk Mitigation and Efficiency

1. RECOMMENDATION: GO, WITH CRITICAL GOVERNANCE PROVISOS

This project is strategically vital. While Halyk Bank's market dominance currently masks significant operational inefficiencies in Legal Compliance, reliance on exhausted manual review is a ticking time bomb for financial and reputational risk. An AI Legal Compliance Assistant offers substantial, quantifiable benefits that outweigh its costs, but success hinges on rigorous adherence to data governance and ethical AI principles within the Kazakhstani legal framework.

2. FINANCIAL JUSTIFICATION & ROI

The proposed AI Legal Compliance Assistant targets a critical bottleneck: the manual review of standard legal documents. For a department of 20 lawyers, each gaining an estimated 10 hours of reclaimed time per week, the projected annual savings in direct labor costs alone are 42,000,000 KZT. Factoring in an estimated annual AI operational cost of 16,200,000 KZT, the project yields a net annual benefit of 25,800,000 KZT. This translates to an impressive first-year ROI of ~159%, indicating rapid payback and sustained cost avoidance. Beyond direct savings, this frees highly skilled legal professionals for complex, value-adding tasks, converting a 'cost center' into a 'strategic risk protector.'

3. GOVERNANCE, ETHICS & RISK MITIGATION

While financially compelling, this project presents significant governance and ethical considerations that are paramount for a financial institution in Kazakhstan:

Data Residency & Confidentiality (HIGH RISK): Processing confidential bank contracts via external cloud-based LLMs (e.g., Google, Claude) whose servers are outside Kazakhstan is a direct violation of the Law "On Personal Data and Its Protection" and the Law "On Banks and Banking Activities" regarding banking secrecy.
Mitigation: The AI solution MUST be deployed either on-premise within Halyk Bank's secure data centers or exclusively on cloud infrastructure physically located within the Republic of Kazakhstan that meets all regulatory requirements. Data must be completely isolated from external models.
Explainability & Transparency (CRITICAL): Legal professionals require full transparency. The AI must not just flag issues but provide precise justifications by referencing specific articles of Kazakhstani law or internal bank policies.
Mitigation: The UI must display AI's reasoning, and a "human-in-the-loop" feedback mechanism is essential for continuous validation and oversight.
AI Hallucination & Accuracy (CRITICAL): The risk of AI generating plausible but incorrect legal interpretations is unacceptable.
Mitigation: The AI's role is strictly an assistant to highlight potential issues; final decision-making remains with the human lawyer. The system must utilize Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG), relying on a constantly updated, verified internal database of laws and bank policies rather than generative AI knowledge. Rigorous pre-deployment and continuous A/B testing with legal experts is mandatory.
Accountability & Bias: Legal responsibility for any errors remains with the human legal professional. Continuous monitoring and auditing of the AI's performance and data sources are required to detect and correct any biases.
4. CONCLUSION & NEXT STEPS

The AI Legal Compliance Assistant is not merely an efficiency tool; it is a strategic investment in operational resilience and risk prevention that can protect Halyk Bank from future liabilities that currently remain invisible. The financial upside is clear. However, the project can only proceed if the significant data governance and ethical AI challenges are addressed with a "security-first, compliance-first" approach.

Recommended Next Steps:

Form a cross-functional task force (Legal, IT/Halyk Lab, Risk Management, Product) to conduct a detailed vendor/solution assessment focusing on Kazakhstani data residency and on-premise/private cloud deployment options.
Develop a robust data strategy for feeding the AI with clean, current, and compliant legal databases.
Initiate a pilot project with a small team of lawyers to validate the AI's accuracy and usability in a controlled environment, simultaneously collecting data to refine the business case.
This project is a GO under the strict condition that a compliant and secure implementation strategy, prioritizing data integrity and human oversight, is firmly established before development commences.
