Loan Agreement No. 2024/03-KLD between JSC "Halyk Bank of Kazakhstan" and Mr. Ivanov I.I.
Date: 15.03.2024.

This Loan Agreement is governed by the legislation of the Republic of Kazakhstan.

1. Subject of the Agreement:
   The Bank grants the Client a consumer loan in the amount of 12,500,000 (twelve million five hundred thousand) KZT.

2. Term and Loan Conditions:
   2.1. Loan Term: 84 (eighty-four) months.
   2.2. Interest Rate: The annual interest rate is set at 22% per annum. The Bank has the right to unilaterally review the rate every 3 months, notifying the Client 5 calendar days in advance.
   2.3. Monthly Payment: 190,000 KZT.
   2.4. Fees: The Client undertakes to pay a monthly fee for maintaining the loan account in the amount of 750 KZT. For full early repayment of the loan, a commission of 3% of the outstanding principal debt is charged.

3. Responsibility of the Parties:
   3.1. For late payment, the Client pays the Bank a penalty of 0.8% of the overdue payment amount for each day of delay, but not more than 15% of the principal debt.
   3.2. Additionally, the Bank charges a penalty for the non-targeted use of funds, if identified.

4. Dispute Resolution:
   All disputes are subject to consideration in the court at the Client's place of residence in Astana.
